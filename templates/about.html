About us

Greenfield was created with a simple idea in mind: make it easier for people to buy fresh, local produce while supporting the farmers and small businesses who grow it. We believe food should feel personal — you should know where it comes from, who produced it, and the story behind every box, bottle, or bunch.

Our platform connects independent producers directly with the community. No long supply chains, no hidden middlemen — just honest food, grown and made with care. Whether it’s seasonal veg, dairy from nearby farms, or handmade bakery goods, everything on Greenfield comes from real people you can discover on our Producers page.

We’re also committed to sustainability. By keeping things local, we reduce food miles, cut down on waste, and help build a greener, more resilient food system. Every purchase supports a producer, strengthens the local economy, and brings you fresher, better‑quality food.
